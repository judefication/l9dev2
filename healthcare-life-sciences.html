Overview

Pharmaceutical products, biological materials, diagnostics, medical devices and clinical research supplies may each require different storage conditions, packaging, handling, documentation and transport. L9 connects the relevant capabilities, specialist partners and workflows around those requirements.

A Requirements-Led Approach

We begin by understanding the product, operating environment and supply chain challenge. Our approach considers:

  • Product characteristics and handling requirements
  • Required storage and transport conditions
  • Origin, destination and distribution scope
  • Relevant quality and regulatory obligations
  • Documentation and information needs
  • Existing providers and systems
  • Operational constraints and service expectations
