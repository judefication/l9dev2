Who We Are

L9 helps healthcare and life sciences organisations build supply chains that are better connected, more visible and more resilient. These chains involve specialised products, many parties and demanding requirements, so we combine supply chain design, partner coordination and practical technology.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To connect healthcare and life sciences supply chains through reliable operations, integrated workflows and intelligent technology.

Vision

To enable a more connected, visible and resilient healthcare and life sciences supply chain ecosystem.
